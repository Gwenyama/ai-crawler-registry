# AI Intelligence Registry

The AI Intelligence Registry is an open, community-maintained registry of AI systems, LLM crawlers, browser agents, AI search engines and verification fingerprints.

Our mission is to become the world's most comprehensive source of truth for identifying AI systems interacting with websites.

## What this project contains

- AI companies
- AI models
- AI crawlers
- AI browser agents
- User-Agent fingerprints
- Browser fingerprints
- Verification methods
- Cloudflare verification metadata
- Published IP ranges
- robots.txt tokens
- AI search engines
- AI training crawlers
- AI retrieval bots
- AI infrastructure providers
- AI capability metadata

## Primary Goals

- Identify every AI visitor to a website.
- Distinguish verified bots from spoofed bots.
- Help website owners understand how AI systems interact with their content.
- Improve AI discoverability and visibility.
- Provide a structured registry that applications can consume programmatically.

## Design Principles
